The problem

AI can propose implementation work. It should not automatically gain authority to change a repository.

A useful plan is not permission to mutate a workspace, run a command, approve a review or ship a delivery.
01 / SEPARATE

Planning from authority

Provider output is typed data. It enters a controlled lifecycle rather than becoming a tool call.

02 / BOUND

Execution to a grant

A distinct scoped grant is required before eligible Runner work in a fixture workspace.

03 / PRESERVE

Uncertainty honestly

Ambiguous external outcomes remain outcome_unknown; they are not auto-retried.

04 / PROJECT

Evidence safely

Reviewable facts are projected without keys, raw traffic, full upstream IDs or billing material.

Evidence-first engineering

Completion claims are backed by a ledger, not a green sentence.

Claims-to-evidence discipline

A claim is accepted only when its source is known and safe to project. Unknowns remain unknown.

Source identity

Verification

Provider lifecycle

Secret-negative evidence

Failure history

Earlier ambiguous attempts remain durable facts, not retroactive success.

Scope limits

Local demo proof is not restated as production deployment or customer use.
